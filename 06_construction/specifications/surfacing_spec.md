## 1. GENERAL

### 1.1 Scope of Works

This Specification governs the supply, placement, containment and maintenance of all playground
surfacing at the six (6) SUIDAC schools. The Works comprise, without limitation:

1. Washed river sand safety surfacing beneath moving equipment and slide runouts.
2. Sand pit edge containment and depth-indication markers.
3. Compacted fine gravel pathway surfacing.
4. Drought-tolerant grass establishment to general play lawn and quiet / shade zones.
5. Stone or recycled tire mulch to tree protection rings.
6. Coarse gravel / rubble surfacing to drainage swales (executed under SUIDAC-SPEC-05).
7. Sampling, testing, protection and defects-period top-up of all surfaces.

All governing technical values are extracted from the **SUIDAC AI Agent Master Command Protocol**,

### 1.6 Banned Materials (Master Command §4.5)

The following shall **not** be supplied or installed under any circumstances:

- **Synthetic turf** — prohibited outright.
- **Rubber tiles** — prohibited, *unless* in the form of recycled tire mulch (permitted only to
  tree protection rings under Clause 3.5).
- **Imported specialty items** — all surfacing materials shall be locally sourced within 50 km.
- **Complex prefabricated structures.**
- **Exotic invasive plant species** — applies to all grass, seed and turf supplied under Clause 3.4.

Any banned material delivered to site shall be rejected and removed from the compound at the
Contractor's expense.

## 2. PRODUCTS AND MATERIALS

### 2.1 Washed River Sand (Safety Surfacing)

**Source:** local riverbed within **50 km of Assosa** (Master Command §4.3, §4.4). The Blue Nile
river-sand source approximately 8 km east of Assosa is identified in the site analysis material
sourcing notes (`README.md` §5 B5) and is the preferred source subject to sampling.

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Description | Clean, washed, free-flowing river sand |
| Particle size range | 0.2–2.0 mm, well graded; nominal single-size 0.5–1.0 mm preferred for impact attenuation [E] |
| Oversize | No particle exceeding 8 mm; no gravel, cobbles or sharp angular stone [E] |
| Silt and clay content | ≤ 3 % by volume, verified by the field jar sedimentation test [E] |
| Organic matter | Nil — no roots, vegetation, shell or refuse |
| Contamination | Free of glass, metal, animal waste and sewage-affected material [E] |
| Fines binding | Sand shall remain loose and free-draining; it shall not cake or bind when dry [E] |
| Compaction | Placed **loose and uncompacted** — safety sand shall never be compacted [E] |

**Field jar test [E]:** a 1-litre sample placed in a clear jar with water, shaken and allowed to
settle for 1 hour shall show a silt layer not exceeding 3 % of the settled sand column depth.

### 2.2 Fine Gravel (Pathways)

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Source | Local quarry within 50 km of Assosa (Master Command §4.3, §4.4) |
| Nominal size | **10–20 mm** (Master Command §4.3) |
| Shape | Angular or sub-angular crushed rock, to interlock under compaction [E] |
| Fines passing 0.075 mm | ≤ 10 % [E] |
| Plasticity index of fines | Non-plastic to PI ≤ 6 [E] |
| Strength / durability | Sound, hard, weather-resistant rock; no shale, no friable material [E] |
| Cleanliness | Free of clay lumps, organic matter and deleterious material |

### 2.3 Grass (General Play Lawn and Quiet / Shade Zones)

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Type | **Drought-tolerant grass** (Master Command §4.3) suited to Assosa's tropical savanna climate at ~1,500 m ASL |
| Method | Transplanted sod / sprigs from an approved local source, or seed (Master Command §4.3) |
| Species control | Locally naturalised, non-invasive species only; **exotic invasive species are banned** (Master Command §4.5) |
| Quality | Free of weeds with rhizomes, disease and pests; sod cut in slabs not less than 40 mm thick [E] |
| Topsoil bed | Re-used site topsoil from SUIDAC-SPEC-01 stockpile, minimum 100 mm depth, scarified into formation [E] |

Species selection shall be confirmed with the Assosa City Administration Education Office and
coordinated with `<S#>_planting_schedule.xlsx` before procurement.

### 2.4 Mulch (Tree Protection Rings)

Either of the following, at **100 mm** depth (Master Command §4.3):

| Option | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Stone mulch | Local stone, 20–50 mm nominal, washed, free of fines [E]; sourced within 50 km |
| Recycled tire mulch | Recycled rubber shred from local waste tires, 10–30 mm nominal [E]; **all steel belt wire completely removed** [E]; free of protruding wire, sharp edges and oil residue |

Recycled tire mulch is the **only** rubber product permitted on this project
(Master Command §4.5).

### 2.5 Swale Rubble / Coarse Gravel

Rubble or coarse gravel, **200 mm** depth, from a local quarry within 50 km
(Master Command §4.3). Nominal size 40–100 mm, hard, sound and free of fines [E].
Placement is specified in SUIDAC-SPEC-05.

### 2.6 Edge Containment Materials

Sand pit and pathway edge restraints shall be formed from one of the following, as shown on
`DWG_sand_pit_edge_detail.dwg`:

| Option | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Treated hardwood or bamboo edging | Treated in full accordance with **SUIDAC-SPEC-04** (boron-based, 24-hour minimum soak) |
| Locally made concrete kerb / edging | Hand-cast C20 concrete, chamfered top arris, no sharp edges [E] |
| Local stone edging | Bedded in C20 concrete haunch, joints flush pointed, no protruding stone [E] |

Edge restraints shall present **no trip hazard and no sharp or splintered edge** to a 5–6 year-old
user, and shall be finished flush with, or set below, the adjacent finished surface level [E].

## 3. EXECUTION

### 3.1 Sequence and Prerequisites

Surfacing shall not commence until:

1. Formation has been compacted to **95 % modified Proctor** and released under SUIDAC-SPEC-01
   Hold Point 2;
2. All equipment footings have been cast, cured and released under SUIDAC-SPEC-03;
3. Drainage swales and outfalls serving the area are complete and functional under
   SUIDAC-SPEC-05;
4. Formation falls of **not less than 1 %** have been verified.

> **HOLD POINT 3 — Pre-surfacing.** The Engineer shall verify formation level, fall, compaction and
> footing top levels before any sand or gravel is placed.

### 3.2 Washed River Sand Safety Surfacing

1. **Extent.** Sand shall be laid over the full **2.0 m clear radius fall zone** measured from the
   edge of each item of moving equipment — E1 Merry-Go-Round, E2 See-Saw and E4 Chain Swing —
   and over the full slide runout of E3 (Master Command §4.1, §4.2).
   For E2 (See-Saw) the surfaced zone is the **8 m × 3 m** total safety zone stated in
   Master Command §4.2.
2. **Depth — moving equipment.** Place washed river sand to a **compacted-in-place-free depth of
   200 mm**, being the upper bound of the specified **150–200 mm** range
   (Master Command §4.3). The 150 mm lower bound is adopted as the **maintenance top-up trigger**
   under Clause 5.3 [E: interpretation of the specified range — placed at maximum, maintained above
   minimum].
3. **Depth — slide runout.** Place washed river sand to **300 mm** depth over the full runout zone
   at the base of E3 (Master Command §4.3). Maintenance top-up trigger: 250 mm [E].
4. **Placement.** Sand shall be barrowed, tipped and spread by hand rake in a single loose layer.
   **Sand shall not be rolled, tamped, vibrated or otherwise compacted.** Placed depth shall be
   controlled by depth pegs set to finished level and withdrawn after levelling [E].
5. **Free-draining base.** The formation beneath sand shall be graded to a **1 % minimum fall** to
   the sub-drain / rubble sump specified in SUIDAC-SPEC-05, so that the sand body **drains within
   24 hours** (Master Command §4.4).
6. **Footings.** The top of every equipment footing shall lie below the sand formation so that the
   full specified sand depth covers the concrete (see SUIDAC-SPEC-03 Clause 3.5). No concrete,
   bolt, anchor plate or fixing shall be exposed within a fall zone.
7. **Edge containment.** Contain sand on all sides in accordance with `DWG_sand_pit_edge_detail.dwg`
   and Clause 2.6, with the edge set flush with adjacent finished levels.
8. **Depth markers.** Install at least one permanent depth-indicator mark per sand zone — a paint
   line or notch on an equipment leg at the specified sand level and at the top-up trigger
   level — to allow non-technical school staff to verify depth [E].

### 3.3 Fine Gravel Pathway Surfacing

1. **Width.** Pathways shall be constructed to a minimum finished width of **1.2 m**
   (Master Command §4.1), maintained wheelchair-accessible where the site permits.
2. **Depth.** Lay fine gravel **10–20 mm** to **100 mm compacted thickness**
   (Master Command §4.3), placed in a single layer, moisture-conditioned and compacted with a
   pedestrian plate compactor to a dense, closed, non-rutting surface [E].
3. **Formation.** Gravel shall be laid on formation compacted to 95 % modified Proctor
   (SUIDAC-SPEC-01) and graded to a cross-fall of 1 % minimum to 2 % maximum [E], never less than
   the **1 %** minimum fall.
4. **Edge restraint.** Provide continuous edge restraint to both sides of every gravel path to
   prevent lateral spread and migration of gravel into sand fall zones [E].
5. **Separation.** Where a pathway abuts a sand fall zone, provide a positive edge and a level
   difference of not more than 20 mm, so that gravel cannot migrate into the sand
   (a gravel stone within a fall zone is an impact hazard) [E].
6. **Finish.** The finished surface shall be free of loose surplus gravel, with no stone standing
   proud, no ruts and no ponding.

### 3.4 Grass — General Play Lawn and Quiet / Shade Zones

1. Spread stockpiled topsoil to a minimum settled depth of 100 mm over scarified formation [E].
2. Establish **drought-tolerant grass** by transplanting sod / sprigs or by seeding
   (Master Command §4.3), to the extents shown on the `planting` drawing.
3. The **quiet / shade zone shall be not less than 20 %** of the total play area
   (Master Command §4.1); grassed and shaded areas shall be measured and recorded to demonstrate
   compliance.
4. Establishment: water daily for the first 21 days and thereafter as required until 80 % ground
   cover is achieved; grass shall be established and rooted before the area is opened to children
   [E].
5. Programme grass establishment to begin at the onset of the wet season (April) wherever the
   construction programme allows, to reduce irrigation demand [E].
6. Bare or failed patches shall be re-sodded or re-seeded during the defects period at the
   Contractor's cost.

### 3.5 Tree Protection Ring Mulch

1. Apply stone mulch or recycled tire mulch to **100 mm** depth over the full tree protection ring
   (Master Command §4.3), the ring extending to the canopy line — nominally 3–5 m radius per
   `README.md` §5 A6 — or as shown on the `planting` drawing.
2. Keep mulch clear of the trunk collar by a minimum of 100 mm to prevent collar rot [E].
3. Do not cultivate, excavate or compact within the protection ring; mulch shall be placed by hand
   over undisturbed ground [E].
4. Where recycled tire mulch is used, the Contractor shall demonstrate by inspection of a
   representative 10 kg sample that all steel wire has been removed before the batch is accepted
   [E].

### 3.6 Drainage Swale Surfacing

Rubble / coarse gravel shall be placed to **200 mm** depth within swales
(Master Command §4.3), in accordance with SUIDAC-SPEC-05.

### 3.7 Dust Suppression and Protection of Finished Surfaces

1. During the **dry season (November–March)**, apply water for dust suppression to bare and
   gravel areas as necessary to prevent airborne dust (Master Command §4.4). Sand safety surfaces
   shall be lightly dampened only; they shall not be saturated or crusted [E].
2. Completed sand zones shall be barricaded against use until equipment commissioning is complete
   under SUIDAC-SPEC-03 [E].
3. Materials shall not be stockpiled on completed surfaces, and barrow runs shall be boarded [E].

### 3.8 Tolerances

| Item | Tolerance |
|------|-----------|
| Sand depth, moving equipment zones | **−0 mm / +25 mm** on 200 mm placed; never below 150 mm in service [E] |
| Sand depth, slide runout | **−0 mm / +25 mm** on 300 mm placed [E] |
| Gravel path compacted thickness | ± 10 mm on 100 mm [E] |
| Gravel path width | **−0 mm / +50 mm** on 1.2 m [E] |
| Mulch depth, tree rings | ± 15 mm on 100 mm [E] |
| Surface regularity under 3 m straightedge (gravel) | 15 mm maximum deviation [E] |
| Level difference between abutting surface types | 20 mm maximum (trip hazard limit) [E] |
| Fall on any surface | Not less than the **1 %** specified minimum |
| Fall zone surfaced radius | **−0 m** on 2.0 m |

Negative tolerance on any Master Command minimum value is not permitted.

### 4.2 Inspection and Testing

| Check | Frequency |
|-------|-----------|
| Sand depth by probe / rod at marked grid points | Minimum 5 points per fall zone [E] |
| Sand cleanliness (visual + jar test) | Per delivery |
| Gravel compacted thickness by test hole | 1 per 25 m of path, minimum 3 per school [E] |
| Path width check | 1 per 10 m of path [E] |
| Mulch depth check | 3 points per tree ring [E] |
| Fall / ponding check after rainfall or flood test | Every surfaced zone, at completion |
| **24-hour drain-down verification** | Every sand zone and path, jointly with SUIDAC-SPEC-05 |
| Fall zone extent check against 2.0 m radius | Every item of moving equipment, 100 % |
| Banned-material audit of all deliveries | Every delivery |

### 4.3 Non-Conformance

Sand found to contain oversize stone, silt above 3 %, organic matter or contamination shall be
removed in full and replaced — partial screening on site is not acceptable [E]. Surfacing laid to
less than the specified depth shall be topped up to the specified depth before acceptance.

## 5. COMPLETION AND MAINTENANCE

### 5.1 Handover Criteria

1. Sand placed to **200 mm** (moving equipment) and **300 mm** (slide runout), verified by probe.
2. Sand fall zones cover the full **2.0 m clear radius** to every item of moving equipment.
3. Pathways constructed at **100 mm compacted** gravel, **1.2 m** minimum width.
4. Tree rings mulched to **100 mm**.
5. Grass established with ≥ 80 % cover; quiet / shade zone ≥ **20 %** of play area demonstrated.
6. All surfaces drain within **24 hours** with no residual ponding.
7. No banned material present anywhere on site.
8. No exposed concrete, bolt or fixing within any fall zone.
9. Per-school safety checklist surfacing items signed off.

### 5.3 Maintenance Regime (for handover to School Maintenance Committee)

| Task | Frequency | Trigger / standard |
|------|-----------|--------------------|
| Rake and level sand; redistribute displaced sand back into fall zones | Weekly during term | Even surface, no scour hollows under swings |
| Check sand depth against depth-indicator marks | Monthly | Top up when depth falls below **150 mm** (moving equipment) or **250 mm** (slide runout) [E] |
| Sand top-up | At least annually, before the wet season | Restore to **200 mm / 300 mm** placed depth |
| Sieve / remove foreign objects, stones and debris from sand | Weekly during term | Sand free of hard or sharp objects |

| Top up tree ring mulch | Annually | Restore **100 mm** depth |
| Mow and water grass; re-seed bare patches | Seasonally | ≥ 80 % ground cover maintained |
| Clear silt from surface / swale interfaces | Before wet season (March) | Free discharge, 24-hour drain-down maintained |
