## 1. GENERAL

### 1.1 Scope of Works

This Specification governs the receipt, protective treatment, foundation, anchorage, erection,
inspection and commissioning of all play equipment at the six (6) SUIDAC schools, for
**pre-primary children aged 5–6 years**. The Works comprise, without limitation:

1. Verification of procured metal equipment (E1–E4) against the specified dimensions and sections.
2. Surface preparation and application of zinc-chromate primer and enamel topcoat.
3. Setting out of equipment positions, fall zones and setbacks from the site datum points.
4. Hand excavation and casting of C20 concrete footings, minimum 300 × 300 × 400 mm per leg.
5. Supply, setting and fixing of anchor bolts and base plates.
6. Erection, plumbing, levelling and final fixing of all equipment.
7. Fabrication and installation of locally constructed elements (L1–L4).
8. Safety inspection, commissioning, and handover of the per-school safety checklist.

## 2. PRODUCTS AND MATERIALS

### 2.1 Steelwork — General

1. All hollow sections shall be new, straight, free of laminations, deep pitting and mill defects,
   and shall match the sections specified in Clause 1.5 [E].
2. Section wall thickness shall be verified on delivery; **RHS 40 × 20 × 1.5 mm** (E1) and
   **pipe 60 × 60 × 2 mm** (E4) thicknesses are minima and shall not be substituted with lighter
   gauge material.
3. Substitution of any specified section requires written approval of the Engineer, supported by
   a check that the substitute is at least equivalent in section modulus and wall thickness [E].
4. All ends of hollow sections shall be sealed by welded cap plates to prevent ingress of water and
   internal corrosion, and to prevent finger entrapment [E].

### 2.2 Welding and Fabrication Quality

1. Welding shall be executed by the **skilled metalworker** in the installation crew
   (Master Command §4.6) using fillet welds of throat thickness not less than the thinner parent
   metal [E].
2. All welds shall be continuous, fully fused and free of undercut, spatter and slag [E].
3. All welds, cut ends and arrises within reach of children shall be **ground smooth**; no sharp
   edge, burr, spike or weld spatter shall remain [E].
4. Radius all exposed corners and edges to a minimum of 3 mm [E].
5. No bolt thread shall project more than 8 mm beyond its nut; longer projections shall be cut back
   and dressed, or fitted with a domed cap nut [E].
6. Openings and gaps in frames shall avoid finger, head and clothing entrapment; where no Ethiopian
   standard applies, the entrapment geometry of the EN 1176 series is recommended as good practice
   for this age group [E — advisory, in support of the "child-safety standard" cited in
   Master Command §4.1].

### 2.3 Protective Coating System (Master Command §4.4)

All metal equipment shall receive **zinc-chromate primer + enamel topcoat**. The following system
applies [dry film thicknesses are E]:

| Stage | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| 1. Surface preparation | Remove all mill scale, rust, oil, grease and weld slag; wire-brush / abrasive clean to bright metal; degrease and dry immediately before priming |
| 2. Primer | **Zinc-chromate primer**, 1 coat minimum, 40 µm DFT [E], applied within 4 hours of surface preparation [E] |
| 3. Topcoat | **Enamel topcoat**, 2 coats, 35 µm DFT per coat [E], total system DFT ≥ 110 µm [E] |
| 4. Recoat interval | As the coating manufacturer's data sheet; each coat fully dry before the next [E] |
| 5. Buried / embedded steel | Additionally apply 2 coats of bituminous paint to all steel from 50 mm above the concrete footing top to the base of the leg, before casting [E] |
| 6. Site touch-up | All site cuts, welds, drill holes and coating damage shall be prepared and re-coated to the full system before commissioning |

Colours shall be as agreed with the School Director and Education Office; lead-based and
chromate-pigmented **topcoats** shall not be used on surfaces accessible to children — the
zinc-chromate specification applies to the primer only, which shall be fully overcoated [E].

Coating shall not be applied in rain, on damp steel, or when the substrate temperature is within
3 °C of the dew point; wet-season (April–October) application requires temporary shelter [E].

### 2.4 Concrete for Footings

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Grade | **C20**, hand-mixed (Master Command §4.6) |
| Nominal volumetric mix | 1 : 2 : 4 (cement : sand : 20 mm coarse aggregate) by volume [E] |
| Water / cement ratio | ≤ 0.55, adjusted for aggregate moisture [E] |
| Slump | 50–75 mm [E] |
| Cement | Ordinary Portland Cement, fresh, stored clear of the ground and dry |
| Fine aggregate | Clean, washed, sharp sand — **not** the safety-surfacing river sand unless tested and approved [E] |
| Coarse aggregate | 20 mm nominal, hard, clean crushed stone from a local quarry within 50 km |
| Water | Clean and potable-quality; free of salt, oil and organics |
| Maximum volume per footing | **0.05 m³** (Master Command §4.6) |
| Batching | By gauge box of known volume; head-pan batching by count is not acceptable [E] |

**Volume check:** the standard footing of 300 × 300 × 400 mm = **0.036 m³**, which is within the
0.05 m³ per-footing limit. Any enlarged footing proposed for ground conditions shall be
re-checked against this limit; the limit shall not be exceeded, and additional capacity shall be
achieved by increasing the number of footings rather than the volume of any one footing [E].

### 2.5 Anchor Bolts and Base Plates

| Item | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Anchor bolts | Cast-in hooked or headed bolts, **M12 minimum**, hot-dip galvanised or zinc-plated [E] |
| Embedment | **200 mm minimum** into the footing, with a minimum 50 mm concrete cover to all faces [E] |
| Number | Minimum **4 bolts per base plate** (leg-mounted equipment) [E], or as detailed on `DWG_footing_detail_300x300x400.dwg` |
| Base plate | Mild steel, 6 mm minimum thickness, welded square to the leg, all edges ground [E] |
| Nuts and washers | Galvanised; **two nuts (locknut) or nyloc nut** per bolt to prevent loosening from vibration [E] |
| Bolt projection | Not more than 8 mm above the nut; dress or cap all projections [E] |
| Setting | Bolts set with a plywood or steel template to guarantee the base-plate hole pattern [E] |
| Alternative | Direct embedment of the leg into the footing is permitted where shown on the drawings, provided the leg is bituminous-coated per Clause 2.3 and fitted with a welded anti-rotation cleat [E] |

Expansion / drop-in anchors into unreinforced hand-mixed concrete shall **not** be used for
moving equipment [E].

### 2.6 Locally Constructed Elements — Materials

1. **Bamboo and timber (L1, L2, L4)** shall be treated in full accordance with **SUIDAC-SPEC-04**
   (boron-based, **24-hour minimum soak**) before installation (Master Command §4.4).
2. **L1 Balance Beam:** treated bamboo of **not less than 80 mm diameter**, or hardwood, finished
   to **L 3.0–4.0 m, H 0.2–0.3 m, W 0.15–0.2 m** (Master Command §4.2).
3. **L2 Climbing Structure:** treated bamboo poles **60–80 mm diameter**, overall height
   **not exceeding 1.2 m** (Master Command §4.2).
4. **L3 Tunnel Crawl:** concrete culvert or corrugated metal pipe, internal diameter
   **0.6–0.8 m**, length **2.0–3.0 m** (Master Command §4.2); both ends free of burrs and sharp
   edges; corrugated metal to be coated per Clause 2.3.
5. **L4 Stepping Logs / Stones:** hardwood timber or local stone, **Ø 0.15–0.2 m**, exposed height
   **0.1–0.15 m**, at **0.3–0.4 m** spacing (Master Command §4.2).
6. All timber surfaces within reach shall be planed or sanded free of splinters; all bamboo nodes
   shall be dressed flush [E].

## 3. EXECUTION

### 3.1 Setting Out and Clearance Verification

1. Set out each equipment centre by dimension from the **two fixed site datum points** on the
   `setting_out` drawing.
2. Before excavation, physically mark on the ground and verify:
   - the **2.0 m clear fall-zone radius** to every item of moving equipment (E1, E2, E4) and the
     **2.0 m base runout** to E3;
   - the **8 m × 3 m** total safety zone for E2;
   - the **1.5 m minimum setback** from any building wall or fence to any equipment edge;
   - the **3.0 m minimum setback** from any building wall to the E4 swing frame or the E3 slide
     platform;
   - that no fall zone overlaps a red (building + 3 m) or orange (fence + 1.5 m) constraint zone;
   - that supervision sight lines from building entrances are unobstructed.
3. Confirm the **1.2 m minimum pathway width** of routes serving the equipment cluster is
   maintained.

> **HOLD POINT 4 — Equipment setting out.** No footing pit shall be excavated until clearances are
> verified on site and released in writing. Overlap of any fall zone with a constraint zone is a
> stop-work condition, and the layout shall be referred back to the Designer.

### 3.2 Footing Excavation

Footing pits shall be hand-excavated to **300 × 300 × 400 mm minimum** per leg
(Master Command §4.6), square, plumb-sided, and with an undisturbed base, under SUIDAC-SPEC-01
Clause 3.6. Loose spoil shall be removed before concreting. Where soft or waterlogged material is
found at the base, the pit shall be deepened and the additional depth made up with compacted
hardcore blinded with 50 mm of lean concrete [E], keeping the structural footing within the
**0.05 m³** limit.

### 3.3 Concrete Footings

1. Mix C20 concrete by hand on a clean, hard, impervious platform — never directly on the ground
   [E]. Mix dry until uniform in colour, then add water gradually to the specified slump.
2. Place concrete within 30 minutes of mixing; concrete showing initial set shall be discarded [E].
3. Place in layers not exceeding 150 mm and compact by hand rodding to eliminate voids [E].
4. Cast each footing in a single continuous operation; construction joints within a footing are not
   permitted [E].
5. Top of footing shall be trowelled level, with the surface **falling away from the leg** by
   approximately 20 mm to shed water [E].
6. **Footing top level:** set so that the full specified surfacing depth covers the concrete —
   in sand fall zones the top of concrete shall be at the sand-pit formation level, giving
   **150–200 mm of sand cover** (300 mm at the slide runout) over the footing
   (Master Command §4.3). Outside fall zones, the top of footing shall be a minimum of 50 mm
   below the finished surface and grassed or gravelled over [E].
   **No concrete, base plate, bolt or anchor shall be exposed within any fall zone.**
7. **Curing:** keep continuously damp under hessian, sand or polythene for a minimum of
   **7 days** [E]. Dry-season (November–March) curing requires daily wetting, twice daily where
   temperatures are high.
8. **Loading:** no equipment shall be loaded, and no child shall be permitted access, until the
   footings have cured for a minimum of **7 days**; full play loading shall not be permitted before
   **14 days** [E].
9. Backfill around footings shall be compacted to **95 % modified Proctor** (SUIDAC-SPEC-01).

> **HOLD POINT 5 — Footings.** Footing dimensions, anchor bolt setting and top-of-footing level
> shall be inspected before backfill and before surfacing is placed.

### 3.4 Erection of Metal Equipment (E1–E4)

1. Equipment shall be erected by the standard crew of **4 unskilled labourers + 1 skilled
   metalworker**, using **manual labour and basic tools only — no heavy machinery**
   (Master Command §4.6). Lifting shall be by hand, with temporary props and guys as necessary.
2. Verify on delivery that each item matches Clause 1.5 dimensions and sections; reject and return
   non-compliant items.
3. **E3 Slide:** the platform height shall not exceed **1.5 m**, within an overall frame of
   **W 3.0 m × H 2.0 m** (Master Command §4.2). The runout shall discharge onto **300 mm** of
   washed river sand. Verify handrails and side containment on the platform and stair before
   commissioning [E].
4. **E4 Chain Swing:** frame **W 2.5 m × H 2.5 m** in **pipe 60 × 60 × 2 mm**
   (Master Command §4.2). Provide a minimum **3.0 m** clear distance from any building wall to the
   swing frame (Master Command §4.1) and a **2.0 m** clear fall zone all around. Chains shall be
   galvanised, shackles seized or welded closed, and seats of flexible or timber construction with
   no rigid protruding corners [E].
5. **E2 See-Saw:** overall **L 4.0 m**, fulcrum height **1.0 m**, in **RHS 40 × 40 mm**
   (Master Command §4.2). The full **8 m × 3 m** safety zone shall be sand-surfaced. Provide
   impact-limiting stops beneath each seat end and hand grips at each seat [E].
6. **E1 Merry-Go-Round:** **Ø 2.3 m × H 1.0 m** in **RHS 40 × 20 × 1.5 mm**
   (Master Command §4.2), on a **2.0 m radius** sand fall zone. The central bearing shall rotate
   freely without wobble; the gap between the rotating platform and any fixed element shall be
   detailed to prevent entrapment of a child's foot or hand [E].
7. All equipment shall be erected plumb and level, with all bolts fully tightened and locked.
8. All moving parts shall be lubricated and free-running before commissioning [E].

### 3.5 Installation of Locally Constructed Elements (L1–L4)

1. All timber and bamboo shall be **fully treated and dried before installation**
   (SUIDAC-SPEC-04); untreated material shall not be built in.
2. Where a treated member is cut, drilled or notched after treatment, the exposed surface shall be
   brush-treated with two coats of boron solution before installation (SUIDAC-SPEC-04).
3. **Avoid direct ground contact** wherever possible: set posts on or in C20 concrete footings with
   the timber standing a minimum of 50 mm above the finished concrete, and coat any below-ground
   or embedded portion with bitumen [E].
4. **L1 Balance Beam:** set to a finished top height of **0.2–0.3 m** above surfacing, level along
   its length, with all fixings countersunk and capped [E].
5. **L2 Climbing Structure:** overall height **shall not exceed 1.2 m**; joints between bamboo poles
   shall be through-bolted or lashed with corrosion-resistant fixings; verify rigidity by applying a
   horizontal service load at the top rail before commissioning [E].
6. **L3 Tunnel Crawl:** bed the culvert / pipe on compacted granular material with the invert level
   or falling at not less than 1 % to prevent water standing inside; both ends dressed smooth;
   ensure the tunnel interior is visible end-to-end for supervision [E].
7. **L4 Stepping Logs / Stones:** set at **0.3–0.4 m** spacing measured between adjacent faces, with
   exposed height **0.1–0.15 m**, each unit set in a concrete collar so that it cannot rock or be
   dislodged [E]. Top faces shall be level and non-slip.

### 3.6 Installation Tolerances

| Item | Tolerance |
|------|-----------|
| Plan position of equipment centre from datum | ± 25 mm [E] |
| Verticality of legs and posts | ± 5 mm per metre of height [E] |
| Level across horizontal members (e.g. swing beam, balance beam) | ± 5 mm over the member length [E] |
| Footing plan dimensions | **+50 mm / −0 mm** on 300 × 300 mm |
| Footing depth | **+50 mm / −0 mm** on 400 mm |
| Anchor bolt embedment | **−0 mm** on 200 mm [E] |
| Fall zone radius | **−0 mm** on 2.0 m |
| Setbacks | **−0 mm** on 1.5 m / 3.0 m |
| Slide platform height | **+0 mm** on 1.5 m maximum |
| L2 climbing structure height | **+0 mm** on 1.2 m maximum |
| L4 stepping unit spacing | Within the 0.3–0.4 m range, no gap exceeding 0.4 m |

Dimensions expressed in the Master Command as maxima (platform 1.5 m, L2 height 1.2 m, footing
volume 0.05 m³) shall not be exceeded; dimensions expressed as minima (footing size, fall zones,
setbacks) shall not be reduced.

### 4.4 Site Safety During Installation

1. Installation areas shall be barricaded and children excluded during working hours [E].
2. Welding and grinding shall be screened; hot work shall not be undertaken within 10 m of an
   occupied classroom during lessons [E].
3. PPE — welding shield, gloves, eye and hearing protection, boots — shall be provided to the crew.
4. Wet-season (April–October) works shall be suspended during electrical storms [E].

## 5. COMMISSIONING, HANDOVER AND MAINTENANCE

### 5.1 Commissioning Criteria

Equipment shall be released for use only when all of the following are demonstrated and recorded on
the per-school safety checklist:

1. Footings cured a minimum of **7 days**; full play loading not before **14 days**.
2. All footings **300 × 300 × 400 mm minimum**, each **≤ 0.05 m³**, cast in hand-mixed **C20**.
3. All anchor bolts fully embedded, tightened and locked; no thread projection exceeding 8 mm.
4. Complete **zinc-chromate primer + enamel topcoat** system applied, with all site damage
   touched up.
5. **2.0 m clear fall zone** to every moving item; **1.5 m / 3.0 m** setbacks satisfied.
6. Sand surfacing in place at **150–200 mm** (moving equipment) and **300 mm** (slide runout), with
   no concrete or fixing exposed.
7. E3 slide platform **≤ 1.5 m**; L2 climbing structure **≤ 1.2 m**.
8. All L-series timber and bamboo boron-treated with documented **24-hour minimum soak**.
9. No sharp edge, protrusion, entrapment gap or splinter anywhere on the installation.
10. Supervision sight lines from building entrances verified clear.
