## 1. GENERAL

### 1.1 Scope of Works

This Specification governs the selection, preparation, preservative treatment, drying, handling and
maintenance of **all timber and bamboo** incorporated into the Works at the six (6) SUIDAC schools.
It applies without exception to:

1. **L1** Balance Beam — treated bamboo (**≥ 80 mm dia.**) or hardwood.
2. **L2** Climbing Structure — treated bamboo poles (**60–80 mm dia.**).
3. **L4** Stepping Logs — hardwood timber (where the timber option is used in place of stone).
4. Sand pit and pathway edge containment in timber or bamboo (SUIDAC-SPEC-02 Clause 2.6).
5. Any post, rail, bollard, seat, shade frame, sign board or other timber or bamboo element,
   whether structural or not, and whether in ground contact or not.

> **Termite risk at Assosa is recorded as HIGH.** No untreated timber or bamboo shall be
> incorporated into any part of the Works.

### 1.5 Prohibited Preservatives

Only **boron-based** preservatives are permitted under the Master Command. The following shall not
be used on this project, being unsuitable for surfaces contacted by 5–6 year-old children [E]:

- Chromated copper arsenate (CCA) and other arsenical preservatives;
- Creosote and coal-tar derivatives;
- Pentachlorophenol;
- Waste engine oil, sump oil and used transformer oil;
- Any unlabelled agricultural pesticide.

### 1.6 Definitions

- **DOT** — disodium octaborate tetrahydrate, the preferred boron preservative salt.
- **BAE** — boric acid equivalent, the standard measure of boron loading.
- **Soak (dip-diffusion)** — full immersion of the member in preservative solution for a specified
  period, followed by a covered diffusion / drying period.
- **VSD** — vertical soak diffusion, a bamboo treatment method using gravity displacement of sap.
- **Green bamboo** — bamboo treated within 48 hours of harvest, while sap is still present.

## 2. PRODUCTS AND MATERIALS

### 2.1 Preservative

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Type | **Boron-based** (Master Command §4.4) — disodium octaborate tetrahydrate (DOT), or a borax / boric acid blend in a 1.54 : 1 ratio by weight [E] |
| Solution strength | **5 % BAE minimum** (approximately 10 % w/w DOT in water) for soak treatment [E] |
| Mixing water | Clean water, warmed to 40–60 °C to aid dissolution; solution to be fully dissolved with no undissolved crystals [E] |
| Solution monitoring | Strength verified by hydrometer / Baumé at the start of each treatment day and topped up as timber removes salt from the bath [E] |
| Supply | Sourced within **50 km of Assosa** where available; otherwise from the nearest Ethiopian supplier — **imported specialty proprietary products are not permitted** (Master Command §4.5) |
| Storage | Dry, covered, off the ground, in labelled containers secured against access by children |

### 2.2 Timber

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Species | Locally available hardwood, non-invasive, sourced within 50 km of Assosa [E] |
| Condition | Sound, free of active insect attack, decay, loose knots, shakes and through-splits [E] |
| Moisture content before soaking | Air-dry, ≤ 25 % [E] — boron diffuses into moist timber but must not be waterlogged |
| Sapwood | Permitted, and shall be fully treated; sapwood is the most termite-susceptible zone [E] |
| Finished surfaces | Planed or sanded free of splinters and sharp arrises; all edges rounded to a minimum 3 mm radius [E] |
| Dimensions | As Master Command §4.2 for L1 and L4 (see Clause 1.4) |

### 2.3 Bamboo

| Property | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Diameter | **≥ 80 mm** for L1 balance beam; **60–80 mm** for L2 climbing poles (Master Command §4.2) |
| Maturity | Culms 3–4 years old at harvest; immature or over-mature culms shall be rejected [E] |
| Harvest season | Harvest in the dry season (**November–March**) when starch content is lowest, reducing borer attack [E] |
| Condition | Straight, free of splits, cracks, borer holes, fungal staining and mechanical damage [E] |
| Treatment window | Treat **within 48 hours of harvest** where green (VSD) treatment is used [E] |
| Node preparation | For soak treatment, internal node diaphragms shall be punctured with a steel rod to allow solution ingress to every internode [E] |
| Ends | Cut square, immediately above a node where practicable, and dressed smooth [E] |

### 2.4 Treatment Bath / Tank

1. A watertight soaking tank of adequate length to fully immerse the longest member —
   L1 balance beam up to **4.0 m** — shall be provided [E]. Acceptable forms include a masonry or
   blockwork trough lined with heavy-gauge polythene, or a steel tank.
2. The tank shall be sited on level, firm ground, clear of drainage lines, swales, wells and
   watercourses, and shall be fenced or lidded and locked against access by children [E].
3. A weighting grid or cross-bars shall be provided to hold members fully submerged; partial or
   floating immersion does not satisfy the 24-hour soak requirement [E].
4. A covered, ventilated, rain-protected stacking area shall be provided adjacent to the tank for
   the post-treatment diffusion and drying period [E].

## 3. EXECUTION

### 3.1 Preparation Before Treatment

1. **All cutting, drilling, notching, planing and shaping shall be completed before treatment**
   [E]. Members shall be cut to final length and all fixing holes drilled before immersion, so that
   the preservative penetrates all exposed surfaces.
2. Remove bark completely from all timber; bark harbours borers and prevents penetration [E].
3. Clean off soil, mud and debris; members shall be free of surface contamination before immersion.
4. Puncture bamboo node diaphragms as Clause 2.3.
5. Batch and tag members so that each batch can be traced through the treatment record
   (Clause 4.2).

### 3.2 Soak Treatment — Primary Method

1. Fill the tank with boron solution at the strength specified in Clause 2.1.
2. Fully immerse all members, weighted below the solution surface with a minimum of 50 mm of
   solution cover over the highest member [E].
3. **Maintain full immersion for a minimum of 24 hours continuous**
   (Master Command §4.4). The soak clock starts only when the last member of the batch is fully
   submerged and weighted.
4. **Recommended enhanced soak [E]:**

   | Member | Minimum soak (Master Command) | Recommended soak for HIGH termite risk |
   |--------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
   | Bamboo poles, punctured nodes (L1, L2) | 24 hours | 72 hours |
   | Sawn hardwood ≤ 50 mm thick | 24 hours | 48 hours |
   | Hardwood sections > 50 mm thick, stepping logs (L4) | 24 hours | 7 days |
   | Members in or near ground contact | 24 hours | 7 days |

   The 24-hour figure is an **absolute minimum** and shall never be reduced; longer soaks improve
   diffusion depth and are to be programmed wherever the construction sequence allows.
5. Record the immersion start time, removal time, batch identity, solution strength and ambient
   conditions for every batch (Clause 4.2).
6. Solution shall be topped up and its strength re-verified between batches; depleted solution shall
   be recharged, not merely diluted with water [E].

### 3.3 Vertical Soak Diffusion — Alternative for Green Bamboo [E]

Where bamboo is treated green within 48 hours of harvest, VSD may be used as an alternative to full
immersion, subject to the Engineer's approval:

1. Stand culms vertically, cut end up, with all internal node diaphragms punctured except the
   lowest.
2. Fill the hollow culm with boron solution and top up as it descends.
3. Maintain the charge for a minimum of 7 days, then invert and repeat for a further 7 days.
4. Follow with the covered diffusion and drying period of Clause 3.4.

VSD does not exempt any member from the recording, verification and end-sealing requirements of
this Specification.

### 3.4 Post-Treatment Diffusion and Drying

1. On removal from the tank, allow members to drain over the tank so that surplus solution is
   recovered [E].
2. **Diffusion period:** close-stack the treated members, cover with polythene sheeting and keep in
   the shade for a minimum of **14 days** to allow the boron to diffuse into the section [E].
3. **Drying period:** open-stack the members on bearers clear of the ground, under cover and with
   free air movement, until air-dry — typically a further **14–28 days** [E]. Members shall not be
   dried in direct sun, which causes checking and splitting of bamboo.
4. **Treated members shall not be rained on before installation.** Boron is water-soluble and will
   leach from wet, unsealed timber (Master Command records ~1,200 mm annual rainfall, wet season
   April–October) [E].
5. Treated material shall be stored under cover on site, clear of the ground and clear of standing
   water, until fixed in position.

> **HOLD POINT 6 — Treatment verification.** No timber or bamboo shall be delivered to the
> installation area or built into the Works until the batch treatment record and penetration test
> result (Clause 4.3) have been submitted and accepted.

### 3.5 Cut Ends and Site-Worked Surfaces

1. Cutting, drilling or notching of treated members after treatment shall be avoided.
2. Where unavoidable, **every newly exposed surface shall receive two flood coats of boron solution
   by brush**, applied on site and allowed to dry before installation [E]. This requirement is
   mandatory and is not a substitute for the original 24-hour soak.
3. The site supervisor shall keep a labelled brush-treatment container of boron solution at the
   work face for this purpose, secured against access by children [E].

### 3.6 Sealing and Weather Protection

1. After drying, all treated timber and bamboo exposed to weather shall be sealed with a
   water-repellent finish — clear or pigmented exterior varnish, paint, or boiled linseed oil —
   to limit boron leaching in the wet season [E].
2. End grain shall receive an additional coat, being the principal route of both moisture entry and
   preservative loss [E].
3. The sealer shall be non-toxic when cured and lead-free, and shall not produce a slippery surface
   on walking or climbing elements [E].
4. Sealing shall not be applied until the member is fully dry; sealing wet timber traps moisture and
   promotes decay [E].

### 3.7 Detailing to Reduce Ground Contact

Boron treatment alone does not confer permanent ground-contact durability; the following detailing
is mandatory [E]:

1. Set posts on or in **C20 concrete footings** (SUIDAC-SPEC-03) with the timber standing a minimum
   of **50 mm above finished concrete level**, the concrete being formed with a fall to shed water.
2. Coat any embedded or below-ground portion with two coats of bituminous paint after boron
   treatment and drying.
3. Do not embed timber or bamboo directly into soil.
4. Locate timber elements clear of drainage swales and low points; no timber element shall stand in
   water that persists longer than the **24-hour** drain-down requirement (Master Command §4.4).
5. Maintain a minimum 100 mm clearance between timber and any accumulating mulch, sand or soil [E].

### 3.8 Termite Management at the Interface

1. All root, stump and woody debris shall have been removed from the formation under
   SUIDAC-SPEC-01 Clause 3.3 before timber elements are installed.
2. No timber offcuts, formwork, packaging or wood waste shall be buried on site or left within the
   compound; all waste wood shall be removed at the end of each working day [E].
3. Any active termite mound found within the play area shall be reported to the Engineer, fully
   excavated including the queen chamber, and the void backfilled with compacted engineered fill
   [E].

### 4.2 Treatment Record

A record shall be kept for every batch and submitted to the Engineer. Minimum content:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Batch reference | S3-BAT-04 |
| Member description and quantity | 12 nr bamboo poles, 70 mm dia., L2 climbing structure |
| Species / material | Bamboo, harvested 2026-01-14 |
| Solution strength at loading | 5.2 % BAE |
| Date and time of full immersion | 2026-02-03, 08:15 |
| Date and time of removal | 2026-02-04, 09:30 |
| **Soak duration achieved** | **25 h 15 min (≥ 24 h — compliant)** |
| Diffusion / covered stacking period | 2026-02-04 to 2026-02-18 |
| Drying period and moisture check | 2026-02-18 to 2026-03-06 |
| Penetration test result | Positive to full section — curcumin test |
| Signature of site supervisor | — |

Any batch recording a soak duration of **less than 24 hours** shall be rejected and re-treated in
full. Partial credit for a short soak is not permitted.

### 4.3 Penetration Verification

1. **Curcumin (turmeric) spot test [E]:** on one sacrificial member per batch, cut a fresh cross
   section, apply curcumin reagent followed by salicylic acid–alcohol reagent; boron presence is
   indicated by a red / orange colouration.
2. The test shall demonstrate boron presence **through the full section** for sawn timber up to
   50 mm thickness, and **through the full culm wall** for bamboo [E].
3. Where penetration is incomplete, the whole batch shall be returned to the tank for a further
   soak of not less than 24 hours and re-tested.
4. Frequency: **1 test per batch**, and not less than 1 test per 20 members [E].

### 4.4 Visual Acceptance at Installation

Every member shall be inspected immediately before fixing and rejected if it shows: active insect
attack or frass; borer holes; fungal staining or decay; through-splits or shakes; splinters or sharp
edges; unsealed cut ends; or absence of a valid batch tag [E].

### 4.5 Health, Safety and Environment

1. Boron preservatives are low in mammalian toxicity but shall be handled with gloves, eye
   protection and overalls; operatives shall wash before eating [E].
2. **The treatment area shall be fenced and children excluded at all times.** Tanks shall be lidded
   and locked outside working hours [E].
3. Solution shall not be discharged to the ground, to any swale, watercourse, well or drinking-water
   source. Spent solution shall be recharged and re-used; final residues shall be disposed of in
   accordance with the direction of the Engineer and local authority requirements [E].
4. Drips and spillage shall be contained within a bunded area around the tank [E].
5. Treated offcuts shall not be used as cooking fuel; all offcuts shall be removed from the
   compound and disposed of as directed [E].

## 5. COMPLETION AND MAINTENANCE

### 5.1 Handover Criteria

1. Every timber and bamboo element in the Works is traceable to a batch record demonstrating a
   **boron soak of not less than 24 hours**.
2. Penetration verification is positive for every batch.
3. All post-treatment cut surfaces have received two brush coats of boron solution.
4. All members are dry, sealed, free of splinters, and detailed clear of ground contact.
5. L1 uses bamboo of **≥ 80 mm** diameter (or hardwood); L2 uses poles of **60–80 mm** diameter and
   does not exceed **1.2 m** in height; L4 units are **Ø 0.15–0.2 m** at **0.3–0.4 m** spacing.
6. No untreated timber or bamboo remains anywhere within the compound.
7. The consolidated treatment register has been handed to the school and to IRC.

### 5.3 Maintenance Regime (for handover to School Maintenance Committee)

| Task | Frequency | Standard |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Inspect all timber and bamboo for termite mud tubes, frass, borer holes, softening and splits | Termly, and always at the end of the wet season (October) [E] | No active attack; no soft or hollow-sounding members |
| Re-apply water-repellent sealer / paint | Annually, at the start of the dry season (November) [E] | Continuous unbroken film, end grain fully covered |
| Brush re-treatment with boron solution to weathered, cracked or newly cut surfaces | Every 2 years [E] | Two flood coats, applied dry-season |
| Clear soil, sand and mulch build-up away from timber bases | Termly [E] | Minimum 100 mm clearance maintained |
| Check that timber bases stand clear above concrete and are not standing in water | After the first heavy rains each wet season [E] | No standing water at any timber base |
| Replace any member showing active termite attack or structural softening | Immediately | Replacement member fully treated to this Specification before installation |

Replacement timber and bamboo procured by the school during the maintenance period shall be
treated in strict accordance with this Specification — **boron-based, 24-hour minimum soak** — before
installation.
