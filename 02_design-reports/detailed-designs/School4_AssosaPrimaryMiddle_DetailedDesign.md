# SCHOOL: Assosa Primary & Middle (School 4)
**STUDENTS:** 230 (M:124 F:106)
**SITE AREA:** 50,662.4 m²
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** 46,614.2 m² (Total open), ~1,200 m² (Primary active zone)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
Assosa Primary & Middle is the largest school in the project, both by student population (230) and land area (over 5 hectares). The site contains numerous building blocks and significant open space. Given the high number of students, the current play equipment is inadequate and prone to overcrowding. One swing set requires maintenance, and a non-functional merry-go-round occupies valuable space. The site is relatively flat, but the sheer scale requires a decentralized play strategy to prevent bottlenecking near the main building.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** 3m buffer around the 15+ classroom blocks. Central assembly courtyard (Keep clear for ceremonies).
- **Yellow Zones:** Distant fields (Requires future development for sports).
- **Green Zones:** Two large clearings (each ~600 m²) located near the pre-primary and primary building clusters.
- **Blue Zones:** Major arterial pathways connecting building clusters.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- Space allows for "Multiple Hubs" to distribute 230 students.
- Potential for a large-scale sensory path connecting play hubs.
- Integration of large-scale stepping stone circuits.

## B. SELECTED LAYOUT: Option A (Maximum Play Value)
Given the 230 students, a single play zone is insufficient. Option A for this school involves creating **two separate active hubs** to distribute the load.

**Dimensioned Description:**
- **Hub 1 (Pre-Primary focus):** 25m x 25m, contains new MGR, refurbished swings, and tunnel.
- **Hub 2 (Primary focus):** 25m x 25m, contains See-Saw, Stepping Stones, and balance beams.

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
Both hubs will be graded with a 1% cross-slope. Extensive swale systems are needed between building clusters to ensure the large open spaces do not become marshy during the wet season.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Double Capacity:** See-saws are doubled to handle high student numbers.
- **Foundations:** Increased footing size for MGR (400x400x500mm) due to high usage frequency.
- **Sensory Path:** Stepping stones must be flush with ground level to prevent trip hazards for running children.
