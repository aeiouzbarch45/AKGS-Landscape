# SCHOOL: Gemeharu Primary & Midlevel (School 1)
**STUDENTS:** 68 (M:25 F:43)
**SITE AREA:** 448.7 m² (KG specific zone) within a larger 4.8 ha compound.
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** ~250 m² (Open space within the KG fence)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
Gemeharu is the smallest school in terms of student population (68) and has a dedicated Kindergarten (KG) zone of approximately 448 m² situated within a much larger 4.8-hectare primary school compound. Currently, there is **no play equipment** in this zone. The KG area is fully fenced with corrugated metal. The ground is standard Assosa red dirt. Because the building footprint within this small zone is high (approx. 180 m² if excluding main primary buildings), space is the primary constraint. All play stations must be carefully placed to maintain safety zones in a compact area.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** 1.5m from KG fence. 3m from classroom doors.
- **Yellow Zones:** Area immediately adjacent to the main primary compound gate (High traffic).
- **Green Zones:** The two small clearings (approx. 100 m² each) on either side of the KG building entrance.
- **Blue Zones:** Central path connecting the KG entrance to the main school yard.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- Compact, controlled environment is easier to supervise.
- High impact: Going from zero equipment to a full set will significantly improve student experience.

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
The small yard will be graded to a 1% slope toward the main compound gate. A simple gravel-filled perimeter drain (200mm wide) will be installed along the fence line to prevent water from pooling against the metal sheets.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Space Management:** Safety zones for the slide and see-saw must not overlap. A minimum 2m clearance between all equipment is mandatory.
- **Surface Containment:** Sand must be contained by a 150mm high concrete kerb to prevent spread into the dirt/grass areas.
