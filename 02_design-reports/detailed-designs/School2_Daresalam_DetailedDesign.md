# SCHOOL: Daresalam Primary & Middle (School 2)
**STUDENTS:** 85 (M:40 F:45)
**SITE AREA:** 78,242.7 m²
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** ~500 m² (Targeted active zone near main building)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
Daresalam occupies an extremely large parcel of land (7.8 hectares) according to the geospatial data. However, the current school infrastructure is concentrated in a smaller portion of the site. The existing play facilities are minimal, consisting of only one functional slide that requires repainting. A non-functional merry-go-round occupies space and must be removed. The site is assumed to be a standard flat compound typical of Assosa, with corrugated metal perimeter fencing. Due to the lack of detailed building data in the KML, a site survey is flagged as a high priority, but design proceeds based on a ~500 m² zone adjacent to the primary classroom building.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** 3m around assumed classroom building. Area of non-functional MGR (Removal zone).
- **Yellow Zones:** The vast majority of the 7.8-hectare site (Future agricultural or sports development).
- **Green Zones:** A 25m x 20m area identified for immediate play development near the school entrance/classrooms.
- **Blue Zones:** Access path from the main gate to the classroom and play hub.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- Unlimited space for future expansion into a district-level sports center.
- Opportunity to establish a high-quality "Play Oasis" in a large, otherwise underdeveloped site.

## B. SELECTED LAYOUT: Option A (Maximum Play Value)
With 85 students, a single well-equipped hub is appropriate. Option A provides a full suite of E-series and L-series equipment to address the significant equipment gap.

**Dimensioned Description:**

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
The hub will be slightly raised (100mm) using local lateritic soil to ensure it remains the highest point in the immediate vicinity, preventing water logging during the wet season.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **MGR Removal:** The old non-functional unit must be completely excavated, including footings, and the hole backfilled with compacted soil before sand installation.
- **Maintenance:** The existing slide must be wire-brushed to remove loose paint/rust, primed, and repainted bright blue to match project standards.
