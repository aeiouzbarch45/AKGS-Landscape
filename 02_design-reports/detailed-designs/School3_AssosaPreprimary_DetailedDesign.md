# SCHOOL: Assosa Preprimary (School 3)
**STUDENTS:** 134 (M:78 F:56)
**SITE AREA:** 5,234.4 m²
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** 4,594.7 m² (Total open), ~450 m² (Targeted active zone)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
The Assosa Preprimary school is located in a relatively spacious compound of over 5,000 m², though much of this is occupied by classroom blocks and a significant mature tree belt on the eastern boundary. The site is bounded by the B40 highway to the north, necessitating a dust and noise buffer. The topography shows a gentle slope toward the south/southeast, which dictates drainage patterns. The ground in the central designated play area is currently bare earth, which is prone to dust in the dry season and mud in the wet season. Existing mature trees provide excellent natural shade on the eastern side. Four main classroom buildings are fixed constraints. Existing play equipment (1 slide, 2 balance, 2 swings, 4 ladders) is functional but requires augmentation to meet the needs of the 134 students.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** Building footprints and a 3m setback from the B40 highway/fence line. No permanent equipment within 1.5m of any fence or 3.0m of building walls.
- **Yellow Zones:** The mature tree belt on the east. Preserve trees; suitable for quiet play or nature paths but no heavy excavation.
- **Green Zones:** The ~450 m² open bare earth area south of the main classroom cluster. This is the primary active play zone.
- **Blue Zones:** Access corridors from classroom entrances to the play area and peripheral circulation.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- The large open central area allows for a comprehensive equipment layout.
- Mature trees offer immediate "Quiet/Shade Zones".
- Existing functional equipment can be integrated into the new master plan.

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
The site requires minor grading to create a 1% slope towards the south. A shallow swale (300mm wide) will be excavated along the southern perimeter of the sand pit to direct rainwater away from the equipment foundations.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Earthworks:** 95% modified Proctor compaction for subgrade under sand/gravel.
- **Surfacing:** 0-4mm washed river sand. 10-20mm quarry gravel for paths.
- **Installation:** Footings C20 concrete. Metal equipment primed with zinc-chromate.
- **Wood Treatment:** Boron-based 24-hour soak for all bamboo/timber.
