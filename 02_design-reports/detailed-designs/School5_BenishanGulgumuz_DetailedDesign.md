# SCHOOL: Benishan Gulgumuz Primary & Middle (School 5)
**STUDENTS:** 124 (M:65 F:59)
**SITE AREA:** 14,427.3 m²
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** 13,006.8 m² (Total open), ~600 m² (Primary active zone)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
Benishan Gulgumuz school is characterized by a large site with a high number of small, dispersed classroom buildings. The primary play area is located in a central clearing surrounded by several classroom blocks. The ground is currently compacted red lateritic soil, which is very dusty and lacks cover. Several mature trees are located close to the building entrances, offering excellent shade opportunities. Existing equipment (swing, balance beam) is located near the main building and is functional. The existing metal slide requires significant maintenance or replacement due to rust and sharp edges.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** Areas between buildings where clearance is less than 3m. Footprint of the 10+ small buildings.
- **Yellow Zones:** Large open areas far from classrooms, suitable for future expansion but currently out of direct supervision range.
- **Green Zones:** The central 600 m² area bordered by mature trees and classroom blocks. This is the designated immediate play zone.
- **Blue Zones:** Pathways connecting the multiple building entrances to the central play zone.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- The abundance of mature trees near the classrooms allows for "Shade-Integrated Play".
- The large site area offers the most potential for a sprawling Nature Play circuit in Phase 2.

## B. SELECTED LAYOUT: Option B (Minimum Viable / Phased)
Given the fragmented nature of the site, Option B focuses on establishing a high-quality "Core Play Hub" near the main classrooms, with a clear expansion path.

**Dimensioned Description:**
The core hub is a 25m x 25m zone located in the main central clearing. Equipment is clustered to allow for easy supervision from the classroom porches.

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
The site has multiple minor drainage paths between buildings. Shallow stone-lined swales will be installed to prevent runoff from entering the sand pits. The play hub will be slightly elevated (50mm) relative to the surrounding dirt to prevent ponding.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Refurbishment Spec:** Existing slide must be sanded to bare metal, treated with phosphoric acid rust converter, primed, and coated with 2 layers of industrial enamel.
- **Footings:** All footings 300x300x400mm minimum.
- **Containment:** All sand zones contained by half-buried local stones or logs.
