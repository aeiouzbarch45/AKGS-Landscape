# SCHOOL: Selamber Primary School (School 6)
**STUDENTS:** 68 (M:30 F:38)
**SITE AREA:** 24,332.9 m²
**AVAILABLE PLAY AREA:** 22,873.2 m² (Total open), ~400 m² (Primary active zone)

## A. SITE ANALYSIS
Selamber Primary occupies a very large compound, but the current play area is significantly degraded. The site is characterized by overgrown tall grass and uneven terrain that requires grading. There is a non-functional merry-go-round and a rusted metal arch that must be removed for safety. However, the school has a good amount of functional equipment (2 slides, 1 swing, 2 ladders, 1 balance) which provides a strong baseline. A notable opportunity exists in the form of discarded tires on-site, which can be recycled for landscaping and play elements.

**Constraint Map Description:**
- **Red Zones:** Area of the non-functional merry-go-round and rusted climber (Decommissioning zone). 3m setback from classroom blocks.
- **Yellow Zones:** Overgrown peripheral areas (Requires heavy clearance/mowing).
- **Green Zones:** The relatively level area between the main classroom block and the southern boundary. This will be the consolidated active play zone.
- **Blue Zones:** Circulation paths from the building to the equipment.

**Opportunity Assessment:**
- Tire recycling for border edging and "stepping tire" paths.
- Massive site allows for future large-scale sports fields.
- Consolidation of existing functional equipment into a unified play zone.

## B. SELECTED LAYOUT: Option A (Maximum Play Value)

## F. GRADING & DRAINAGE
Significant site clearance and leveling are required. The play zone will be graded to a 1% slope towards the eastern boundary. The recycled tire border will act as a minor silt trap for the sand pits.

## G. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Tire Recycling:** Tires must be cleaned, checked for protruding wires, and painted with colorful, non-toxic exterior paint. Vertical tires must be buried to 50% depth.
- **Decommissioning:** All rusted metal must be removed from the site to prevent injury.
- **Footings:** All footings 300x300x400mm minimum.
