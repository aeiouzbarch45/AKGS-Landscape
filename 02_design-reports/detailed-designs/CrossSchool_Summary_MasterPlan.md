## 4. LESSONS LEARNED & RISK REGISTER

### Key Risks:
- **Termite Infestation:** HIGH risk for all bamboo/timber elements. Mitigation: Mandatory 24-hour Boron soak and monthly inspections by school caretakers.
- **Seasonal Flooding:** Assosa's heavy wet season can wash away sand pits. Mitigation: Mandatory raised concrete or log kerbs and peripheral swales for all sand zones.
- **Maintenance Sustainability:** Metal equipment prone to rust if not repainted annually. Mitigation: Community training and provision of a "Maintenance Kit" (paint, brush, lubricant) to each school.
- **Material Availability:** Scarcity of washed river sand in peak construction months. Mitigation: Early procurement and site-stockpiling during the dry season.

### Design Principles for Success:
- **Thermal Comfort:** Prioritizing equipment placement under existing mature tree canopies significantly increases the usable hours of the playground.
- **Safety Zones:** Strict enforcement of the 2.0m fall zone ensures compliance with international child safety standards.
- **Local Integration:** Using recycled tires (School 6) and local stone (School 4) reduces costs and increases community ownership of the project.
